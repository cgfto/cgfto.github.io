Our mission is to walk together in community, discussing difficult aspects of our faith and applying the teachings of Jesus in our lives.

The Christian Graduate Fellowship (CGF) was established in 2020 as an offshoot of Power to Change UTSG. It began when a group of graduate students came together to explore challenging questions, such as how to live out their faith in the workplace. During the pandemic, as the Fellowship grew, meetings transitioned from virtual gatherings to in-person gatherings at members' homes. As the group continued to expand, it became evident that a more permanent and spacious meeting place was necessary. In 2022, Grace Toronto Church graciously offered its facilities for the Fellowship’s weekly meetings. By God's grace, the Christian Graduate Fellowship has continued to flourish, growing from a small group of students into a vibrant community of graduate students and young professionals. We are grateful to have become an official affiliate ministry of Grace Toronto Church.

Weekly Gathering

Every Friday evening we come together for a time of fellowship and discussion. The night begins at 7:30 with musical worship (led by our own worship team), followed by a short lecture or video to introduce the night's topic. We then move into smaller groups for deeper discussion and close with a time of prayer. All are welcome, and there's no commitment required to join. We look forward to welcoming you! Our alternating locations

Our Friday gatherings rotate between two spaces in downtown Toronto, and we share the confirmed venue details each week in advance so everyone can plan confidently. This rhythm helps us stay flexible while keeping community accessible, and it allows us to make the best use of each location depending on the week's format, group size, and activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our fellowship is geared primarily to grad students and professionals in their twenties, up to a maximum of age 35. If you are beyond this age, we recommend connecting with Grace Toronto or your local church to see what other ministries are available (e.g., men's/women's ministry, small groups, etc.)

Grad Fellowship runs year round! As many of us are no longer on a semester schedule, we're able to continue running throughout the summer. Typically we take a short break during the Christmas season and in late August, however there are usually unofficial get-togethers during this time.

Our weekly meetings are drop-in style, no registration required! Feel free to invite friends throughout the year. We only ask that if possible, you arrive on-time at 7:30 so our welcome team can unlock the door for you.

We're always looking for volunteers who are willing to serve in our fellowship! Generally this involves 2–3 hours of commitment per month, including arriving 1–2 hours prior to our start time of 7:30pm. Some opportunities include:

  • Worship team — if you play an instrument or can sing, help lead worship with us!
  • A/V team — We're currently looking for help mixing audio or clicking slides
  • Hospitality team — Help to setup chairs and greet people as they enter
  • Social media team — Help promote our fellowship to new people!
